# Product

## Register

product

## Users
MSU teachers and admins grading multiple-choice exams.

## Product Purpose
A fast, accurate, and easy-to-use web-based OMR grading tool that reduces manual grading time.

## Brand Personality
Professional, Academic, Efficient

## Anti-references
Avoid overly complex dashboards, outdated '90s web portal looks, or overly playful/childish interfaces.

## Design Principles
- **Efficiency first**: Prioritize fast scanning and clear grading workflows.
- **Academic professionalism**: Maintain a structured, trustworthy aesthetic suitable for a university setting.
- **Clarity over decoration**: Keep the interface clean and focused on the core task of grading.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard contrast ratios, legible typography, and considerations for color blindness.
